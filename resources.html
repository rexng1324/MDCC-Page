The case process

Follow these steps in order for every case.

Define the problem

Write one question with measurable goals, such as sales growth or market share by a set date.

Analyze the situation

Use the frameworks below to understand the company, the market and the competition.

Choose and justify a strategy

Compare 2 to 3 options, score them against your goals and recommend one.

Plan and present

Show the action plan, budget and expected results in a clear pitch.

Framework cheat sheet

Which tool to use, and when.

FrameworkUse it to
SWOTSummarize strengths, weaknesses (internal) and opportunities, threats (external)
PESTELScan political, economic, social, technological, environmental and legal factors
Porter's Five ForcesJudge industry attractiveness: new entrants, suppliers, buyers, substitutes, rivalry
Segmentation and targetingDefine who the customer is by location, demographics, values and behaviour
Ansoff MatrixGenerate growth options: penetration, new products, new markets, diversification
4 PsPlan product, price, place and promotion for your strategy

Presentation tips

  • Lead with your recommendation, then explain why.
  • One main idea per slide.
  • Use numbers to support every claim.
  • Practise out loud and time yourself.
  • Prepare for judges' questions on cost and risk.
