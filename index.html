What is a case competition?

A case competition gives teams a real or realistic business problem. Teams have a short time to research it, develop a recommendation and pitch it to a panel of judges.

No business experience is needed. You learn the frameworks as you go, and you build teamwork, public speaking and problem-solving skills that help in any future path.

What we do

Every meeting is hands-on, using the same process real competitions use.

Learn the toolkit

Break down problems with SWOT, PESTEL and Porter's Five Forces, then back a strategy with numbers.

Practise real cases

Work through sample cases in small teams and get feedback on your thinking.

Build presentation skills

Turn analysis into a clear pitch and face Q&A from mock judges.

Compete

Test your team against other teams in competitions in Victoria and Vancouver.

How to join

We meet Tuesdays at Lunch in Annex 3. Bring a friend if you like.

Join a team

Practise, then compete

Work through practice cases, then sign up for a competition when your team is ready.
